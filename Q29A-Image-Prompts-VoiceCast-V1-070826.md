# Q29A Image Prompts — Voice Cast (Character-Consistent)

**Question:** What have you always wanted to say to your loved ones?
**Pairs with:** `CHARACTER-BIBLE-VOICE-CAST-V1-062926.md` and `Planning Text to Speech Experiment.docx`.

## How to generate
For each option, in **Gemini 2.5 Flash Image (Nano Banana)**: **attach the assigned character's image**, then paste the scene prompt (the indented block). The character's identity is written into the prompt, but the attached image is what locks the face across all five questions. Supporting people in a scene are extras (not locked) — they can be anyone.

> **⚠️ NO LEGIBLE TEXT IN ANY IMAGE.** AI renders text poorly. So: no forms, charts, documents, receipts, books, signs, or named wristbands; screens are **glow-only with no readable interface**; papers are **blank, closed, turned away, or folded shut**; photographs within scenes are **soft and indistinct**. The story is told through faces, hands, objects, and staging — never written words.

Each scene is built from the option's **"How This Sounds"** narrative (richer, specific imagery — not just the label).

**Casting:** CP1 → Annakay, Fortunate, Joefe, Eva, Tia, Leoni · CP2 → Fortunate, Eva, Annakay, Tia · CP3 → Joefe, Leoni, Annakay, Tia
