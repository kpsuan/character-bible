# Q23 Image Prompts — Voice Cast (Character-Consistent)

**Question:** When might you consider voluntarily stopping eating & drinking (VSED)?
**Pairs with:** `CHARACTER-BIBLE-VOICE-CAST-V1-062926.md` and `Planning Text to Speech Experiment.docx`.

## How to generate
For each option, in **Gemini 2.5 Flash Image (Nano Banana)**: **attach the assigned character's image**, then paste the scene prompt (the indented block). The character's identity is written into the prompt, but the attached image is what locks the face across all five questions. Supporting people in a scene are extras (not locked) — they can be anyone.

> **⚠️ NO LEGIBLE TEXT IN ANY IMAGE.** AI renders text poorly. So: no forms, charts, documents, receipts, books, signs, or named wristbands; screens are **glow-only with no readable interface**; papers are **blank, closed, or turned away**. The story is told through faces, hands, objects, and staging — never written words.

> **Tone guard for this question:** VSED is heavy subject matter. Every scene stays **dignified and quiet** — no distressed bodies, no graphic thirst or decline. The weight is carried by faces, hands, food, and light.

Each scene is built from the option's **"How This Sounds"** narrative (richer, specific imagery — not just the label).

**Casting:** CP1 → Joefe, Eva, Leoni, Fortunate, Tia · CP2 → Annakay, Tia, Leoni, Fortunate · CP3 → Tia, Eva, Joefe, Fortunate

## Notes
- **No legible text** is enforced in every prompt (glow-only screens/monitors, blank/closed papers and folders). If a generation still produces garbled text, regenerate or add "plain, no writing, no signage" to the offending object.
- **Tone:** all Q23 scenes deliberately avoid depicting bodily distress; the subject's weight is carried through food, hands, empty chairs, and light. Keep it that way if regenerating.
- **PPR patterns & Reflection Guidance** are voiced in the TTS doc but are reflective/text content; the image system illustrates options/checkpoints, so they're not given scene images here.
- Each character keeps her master film stock (Tia/Joefe/Eva → Portra 400; Fortunate/Annakay/Leoni → Pro 400H) for tonal continuity.
