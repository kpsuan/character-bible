# Q11 Image Prompts — Voice Cast (Character-Consistent)

**Question:** How important is physical comfort or being pain-free for you?
**Pairs with:** `CHARACTER-BIBLE-VOICE-CAST-V1-062926.md` and `Planning Text to Speech Experiment.docx`.

## How to generate
For each option, in **Gemini 2.5 Flash Image (Nano Banana)**: **attach the assigned character's image**, then paste the scene prompt (the indented block). The character's identity is written into the prompt, but the attached image is what locks the face across all five questions. Supporting people in a scene are extras (not locked) — they can be anyone.

> **⚠️ NO LEGIBLE TEXT IN ANY IMAGE.** AI renders text poorly. So: no forms, charts, documents, receipts, books, signs, or named wristbands; screens are **glow-only with no readable interface**; papers are **blank, closed, or turned away**; medication containers are **plain and unlabeled**. The story is told through faces, hands, objects, and staging — never written words.

Each scene is built from the option's **"How This Sounds"** narrative (richer, specific imagery — not just the label).

**Casting:** CP1 → Tia, Joefe, Annakay · CP2 → Eva, Leoni, Fortunate, Tia, Annakay, Joefe · CP3 → Fortunate, Eva, Annakay, Tia, Joefe, Leoni
